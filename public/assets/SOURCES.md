# Preview assets

- `app-icon.svg`, `app-mark.svg`: original Quota Peek Needle design selected by the user. These files are the canonical vector sources. PNG/ICO assets in `src-tauri/icons/` are regenerated by `scripts/generate-icons.mjs`; intermediate exports go to `.cache/icon-exports/`.

- `antigravity.svg`: Google Antigravity, full-color icon from the official press kit: https://antigravity.google/press ; file: https://antigravity.google/assets/image/brand/antigravity-icon__full-color.svg
- `codex.svg`: Codex logo vector from Lobe Icons (third-party distribution, not an official OpenAI asset download): https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/codex.svg
- `claude.svg`: Claude logo vector from Lobe Icons (third-party distribution): https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/claude-color.svg
- Lobe Icons license: `lobe-icons-LICENSE`; upstream https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/LICENSE
- `Geist-Variable.ttf`, `GeistMono-Variable.ttf`: Google Fonts distribution of Vercel Geist and Geist Mono. Licenses: `Geist-OFL.txt`, `GeistMono-OFL.txt`. Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geist and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/geistmono

Brand marks identify their respective providers in this local UI prototype. Their trademarks remain owned by their respective owners; the icon library license does not transfer trademark rights.
